Canada Fishing

Fishing in Canada is unlike anything else. Getting lost in the beauty of nature and disconnecting from technology is one of lifes treasures. Finding the enjoyment of having no distractions of social media and news is a great break. We sometimes find ourselves getting lost in the chaos of the world with access to everything at our finger tips. Taking a trip to Halleys camp in Canada allows you to fully disconnect, and just appreciate simpler things.

Travel Tip

Take only the necesseties. Clothes, hygiene items and medical supplies. Halleys Camp provide boats for rent supplies for cooking on the shores of the lake, and even breakfast. So grab a couple Pizzas for sitting around the table playing card games and leave distractions at the cabin.

Montana Family Trip

Families flock to the Big Sky region of Montana for a relaxing beautiful getaway. My family decided one year that it would be a great vacation to experience. So my parents planned this trip to the mountains, loaded the car up and took us away to one of the most memorable destinations I can remember. Taking this trip allowed me to go explore hiking trails and discover the hidden gems of the forests and mountains. I loved how crisp and fresh the air was the aroma was almost sweet. I recall how much fun I hiking and exploring the region!
